# Perfetti Van Melle (PVM) — Executive Geo-Intelligence Explorer™

## 🚀 Overview
Standalone C-Suite Geospatial & Commercial Intelligence dashboard customized for **Perfetti Van Melle Bangladesh** (Confectionery Division: Mentos, Alpenliebe, Chupa Chups, Center Fresh, Center Fruit).

---

## 🧭 Dual-Mode Executive Navigation
The portal features two synchronized intelligence engines:

1. **🏬 Retail Commercial Network (4,722 Outlets)**
   - 3-Tier Outlet Matrix: 🟢 Active Buyers, 🟡 Visited Blindspots, 🔴 Untapped Stores.
   - 3D Dynamic Sales Height Pillars extruded by revenue volume.
   - Thana & Union GIS micro-drilldown (Demra, Khilgaon, Sabujbagh, Rampura, Motijheel, etc.).
   - Full 4M Audit Revenue: ৳5,744,100.12 across 8,853 orders.

2. **🎓 Institutional Opportunity & Gap Analysis (School & College Footfall)**
   - **5,601 Educational Institutions** in Dhaka Division (2,166 in Dhaka & Narayanganj).
   - **Catchment Buffer Rings:** Interactive 100m (Gate), 200m (Tiffin Zone), 300m, 500m walking radius.
   - **Proximity Gap Classification:**
     - 🔴 **Opportunity Gaps:** 1,917 hubs (88.5%) have **ZERO PVM Outlets** within 200m!
     - 🟡 **At-Risk Hubs:** 239 hubs with outlets in radius, but 0 sales conversion.
     - 🟢 **Covered Hubs:** 10 hubs with active ordering buyers.
   - **Onboarding Lead Generator & Modal:** Click any school to view institution details, distance to nearest outlet, Google Maps link, and commercial deployment recommendations.
   - **Exportable Opportunity Leads:** 1-Click CSV export of targeted schools for SR field route expansion.

## ⚡ How to Run
Simply double-click:
```cmd
start.bat
```
Or open terminal in this folder and run:
```cmd
python -m http.server 8080
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:8080/index.html` in your browser.
